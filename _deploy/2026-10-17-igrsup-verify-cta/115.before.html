What is IGRSUP? The Full Form and Purpose

IGRSUP stands for Inspector General of Registration and Stamps, Uttar Pradesh. (Do not confuse it with IGRS, the Integrated Grievance Redressal System — the separate Jansunwai complaints portal.) It is the official UP registration portal operated by the Stamp and Registration Department, accessible at igrsup.gov.in. Launched to digitise property transactions across the state, the portal has become the single most important government tool for any buyer, seller, or legal professional dealing with property in Uttar Pradesh.

For Lucknow buyers specifically, IGRSUP is the master database of every registered property transaction that has occurred in the five Sub-Registrar Offices covering the district — Lucknow Sadar, Sarojini Nagar, Malihabad, Mohanlalganj, and Bakshi Ka Talab. Any sale deed, mortgage, gift deed, lease, or partition registered at any of these SROs since approximately 2005 is searchable and verifiable online, often in under three minutes and at no cost.

Many buyers mistake IGRSUP for UP Bhulekh, but the two portals serve very different functions. UP Bhulekh (upbhulekh.gov.in) shows the Khatauni — the current land ownership record with khasra and gata details, mainly for rural and agricultural land. IGRSUP, on the other hand, shows the registered document history — every sale deed and transaction officially recorded with the government. Serious property verification requires checking both portals, never just one.

IGRSUP Property Search — Step-by-Step Tutorial for Lucknow

The property search feature — called Sampatti Vivaran (Property Details) — is the most used service on IGRSUP and completely free to access. Here is the exact process to search any Lucknow property online:

1
Visit the Official IGRSUP Website

Open your browser and go to igrsup.gov.in. The homepage appears in Hindi by default. On the left-side menu, locate the section labelled Sampatti Vivaran (सम्पत्ति विवरण) or Property Details. This is the entry point to the search feature. Note: When we checked igrsup.gov.in on 17 October 2026, the property search links opened a User Login page (user ID, password and captcha). If you do not have an account, use the Register Now option on the home page first.

📍 Official URL: https://igrsup.gov.in → Sampatti Vivaran / Property Details
2
Choose Urban or Rural Property Search

The next screen presents two search options: Rural Properties and Urban Properties. The two use different search parameters and pull from different databases. For Lucknow, areas like Gomti Nagar, Indira Nagar, Aliganj, Hazratganj, and Aminabad fall under urban property search. Village plots in Mohanlalganj, Malihabad, Bakshi Ka Talab, Kakori, and rural Sarojini Nagar areas fall under rural property search. Choose incorrectly and you get zero results — which does not mean the property does not exist, it means you searched in the wrong database.

📍 Rule: Urban = LDA/LMC areas | Rural = Gram Panchayat/Revenue Dept villages
3
Fill Rural Property Search Details

If searching a rural Lucknow property, enter: District (select Lucknow), Tehsil / SRO Office (Malihabad, Mohanlalganj, Bakshi Ka Talab, or Sarojini Nagar as applicable), Village / Mohalla name, and the Khasra Number / Gata Number / Plot Number. The khasra must exactly match the official government record — one digit wrong and the search returns nothing. Verify the exact khasra on upbhulekh.gov.in first to avoid wasted searches.

📍 Tip: Cross-check khasra on upbhulekh.gov.in before searching IGRSUP
4
Fill Urban Property Search Details

For urban Lucknow properties, the search uses fewer fields: District (Lucknow) and the Property ID. Property ID is typically available on the property tax receipt from Lucknow Municipal Corporation (LMC) or on the LDA allotment document. If you do not have the Property ID, you can alternatively navigate to the IGRSUP Index feature which allows search by address, tehsil, colony, and mohalla name for properties registered after December 5, 2017.

📍 Property ID source: LMC tax receipt, LDA allotment letter, or previous sale deed
5
Enter Captcha and Submit

Fill in the captcha code exactly as shown and click Submit. The search may take a few seconds. If any registered document matches your property details, the results page displays all registered transactions — sale deeds, mortgages, gifts, partitions — with document numbers, dates, and party names. For properties with 10–15 transactions of history, the result list may span several entries.

📍 Typical result time: 3–10 seconds for most searches
6
Review and Verify the Ownership Chain

Read the transaction list from oldest to newest. Confirm that: (a) the current seller's name appears as the most recent owner after any sale deeds, (b) there are no unresolved mortgages — if a mortgage appears, a subsequent release or discharge must also appear, (c) the chain of transactions is continuous without any suspicious gaps. Any break in the chain, any unresolved mortgage, or mismatch between the seller and the IGRSUP owner name is an immediate red flag.

📍 Save or screenshot the result page for your legal records

IGRSUP Index Search — Deeds Registered After 5 December 2017

If you do not have the khasra number or the property ID, use the IGRSUP Index. It lets you search deeds registered after 5 December 2017 by address details — tehsil, colony and mohalla name — instead of a property ID.

  • Use it when you only know the locality, not the khasra number or property ID.
  • It covers deeds from 5 December 2017 onwards. For older deeds, use the Sampatti Vivaran search by khasra or property ID, or ask at the Sub-Registrar Office.
  • As with the main search, match the seller’s name and the khasra or plot details in the result with the documents you were given.

Stamp Duty & Registration Charges Lucknow 2026 (via IGRSUP)

After searching a property, most Lucknow buyers next use the IGRSUP stamp duty calculator to plan their budget. The current rates as per UP Stamp Duty Act and the UP Cabinet notification of July 2025 are:

Lucknow Stamp Duty & Registration Charges 2026

  • Male Buyer — 7% stamp duty + 1% registration fee = 8% of property value
  • Female Buyer — 6% stamp duty on property up to ₹1 crore (7% above that) + 1% registration = 7% total
  • Women Rebate (July 2025 notification) — the women's 1% concession limit was raised from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore on 22 July 2025
  • Joint Buyers (Husband + Wife) — 6.5% stamp duty + 1% registration
  • Gift Deed between Blood Relatives — Flat ₹5,000 stamp duty (major concession)
  • Base for calculation — Higher of transaction value OR government circle rate for that locality

The IGRSUP stamp duty calculator auto-computes these rates based on your inputs. The critical point buyers miss is that stamp duty is calculated on the higher of transaction value or circle rate, under Section 17 of the Registration Act 1908. If you purchase a plot for ₹40 lakh but the LDA circle rate values it at ₹50 lakh, you pay stamp duty on ₹50 lakh — regardless of what you actually paid the seller.

5 Common IGRSUP Property Search Mistakes

🚨 Mistake 1 — Searching Urban Database for a Rural Khasra

This is the single most common mistake. A buyer looking at a plot in rural Mohanlalganj enters the urban property search, gets no results, and wrongly concludes the property has no registered history. In reality, the correct database was rural. Always confirm the property's administrative zone before selecting Urban or Rural search.

🚨 Mistake 2 — Wrong Tehsil Selection

Lucknow district has multiple tehsils and Sub-Registrar Offices. A property in Sushant Golf City belongs to Sarojini Nagar tehsil, not Lucknow Sadar. Selecting the wrong tehsil returns no results even when the property has full registered history. Cross-check the exact tehsil on upbhulekh.gov.in before filling the IGRSUP search form.

🚨 Mistake 3 — Khasra Number Digit Error

Khasra numbers in Lucknow villages can be 3 to 5 digits with subdivisions (like 234/1 or 234/1/2). Copying the khasra from a photo, old paper, or memory instead of the official Khatauni record leads to digit errors. A single wrong digit returns a blank search result, giving false assurance that the property has no issues. Always copy the khasra directly from the official Bhulekh record.

🚨 Mistake 4 — Trusting a Blank IGRSUP Result Too Quickly

A blank or empty result on IGRSUP does not automatically mean the property is unregistered. It could mean (a) the search parameters were wrong, (b) the property is registered under a slightly different village or colony spelling, or (c) the transaction is from before 2005 and records have not been fully digitised. For older properties especially, a blank result warrants a manual search at the Sub-Registrar Office — not blind trust.

🚨 Mistake 5 — Not Cross-Checking with UP Bhulekh

IGRSUP shows registered document transactions. UP Bhulekh shows current land record ownership. If the seller on IGRSUP and the owner on UP Bhulekh are different names, it usually means a recent transaction has not yet reflected in one of the portals. This mismatch is a red flag that requires explanation before any payment. Both portals must be checked for every verification.

Why IGRSUP Alone is Not Complete Property Verification

IGRSUP is a powerful first filter but not a complete verification by itself. A property can have a clean IGRSUP record and still carry serious risks invisible to the portal:

IGRSUP does not show whether the colony is on LDA's 241+ illegal colony list — that requires a separate check at ldalucknow.in. It does not reveal pending civil cases against the seller in Lucknow District Court. It does not disclose NHAI highway acquisition corridors or AAI airport height restrictions near Kanpur Road. It does not flag Master Plan 2031 land use conflicts (industrial land being sold as residential). And for under-construction apartments, IGRSUP does not verify UP RERA registration — that requires a separate check at up-rera.in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IGRSUP and what is its full form?

IGRSUP stands for Inspector General of Registration and Stamps, Uttar Pradesh. It is the official online portal of the UP Stamp and Registration Department, accessible at igrsup.gov.in. The portal digitises property registration, stamp duty payment, property search, encumbrance certificate applications, e-stamp verification, and marriage registration for all districts of Uttar Pradesh including Lucknow.

How do I search a property on IGRSUP in Lucknow?

Visit igrsup.gov.in and click on Sampatti Vivaran or Property Details. Choose between Rural Properties and Urban Properties based on where the plot is located. For rural properties enter district, tehsil, village name, and khasra number. For urban properties enter district and Property ID. Fill captcha and submit. The portal displays all registered transactions for that property. When we checked igrsup.gov.in on 17 October 2026, the property search links opened a User Login page (user ID, password and captcha). If you do not have an account, use the Register Now option on the home page first.

Is IGRSUP property search free to use?

Basic property search on IGRSUP has been free to use. When we checked igrsup.gov.in on 17 October 2026, the property search links opened a User Login page (user ID, password and captcha). If you do not have an account, use the Register Now option on the home page first. After that, you only need the property details like district, tehsil, village, and khasra number for rural properties, or district and Property ID for urban properties. Paid services on IGRSUP include property registration fees, stamp duty payments, encumbrance certificate applications (Rs.200 to Rs.500), and certified copy requests.

What is the difference between IGRSUP and UP Bhulekh?

IGRSUP (igrsup.gov.in) is the Stamp and Registration Department portal that shows registered document transactions — every sale deed, mortgage, gift, and partition that has been officially registered. UP Bhulekh (upbhulekh.gov.in) is the Revenue Department portal that shows current land ownership through the Khatauni record with khasra and gata details. For complete verification, both portals must be checked — IGRSUP for transaction history and UP Bhulekh for current ownership status.

How far back does IGRSUP property search data go?

IGRSUP digital property records reliably cover transactions registered from approximately 2005 onwards, with the most comprehensive data available from 2017. For older pre-2005 transactions, a manual search at the Sub-Registrar Office is required as paper records have not been fully digitised. For 30-year Encumbrance Certificates covering older periods, buyers should budget additional time for physical record verification at the SRO. The IGRSUP Index feature separately covers properties registered after December 5, 2017 and allows search by address.

What are the stamp duty and registration charges in Lucknow 2026?

In Lucknow 2026, stamp duty is 7 percent for male buyers and 6 percent for female buyers on property up to Rs 1 crore (7 percent above that). Joint buyers (husband and wife) pay 6.5 percent. Registration charge is a flat 1 percent for all buyers. Women get an additional 1 percent rebate on properties up to Rs.1 crore as per July 2025 UP Cabinet notification. Gift deeds between blood relatives carry a flat Rs.5,000 stamp duty. Stamp duty is calculated on the higher of the transaction value or the government circle rate for that locality.

Can I verify an e-stamp certificate on IGRSUP?

Yes, IGRSUP has an e-stamp verification tool called E-Stamp Satyapan. Click on E-Stamp Verification on the portal, which redirects to the SHCIL (Stock Holding Corporation of India Limited) portal. Enter the Unique Identification Number (UIN) from the e-stamp certificate, the issue date, and the duty type. Fill captcha and click Verify. The system confirms whether the e-stamp is genuine, cancelled, or invalid. This takes under 30 seconds and catches forged stamp papers instantly. This check is essential before accepting any property document that claims to be stamped.

Which Sub-Registrar Office should I select for my Lucknow property?

Lucknow district has five Sub-Registrar Offices: Lucknow Sadar (central Lucknow including Hazratganj, Aminabad), Sarojini Nagar (southern areas including Sushant Golf City, Shaheed Path, Vrindavan Yojna), Malihabad (western rural belt), Mohanlalganj (southern rural belt), and Bakshi Ka Talab (northern rural belt). Use the Know Your Office feature on IGRSUP by entering district, tehsil, village, and colony to find the correct SRO. Selecting the wrong SRO is the most common cause of failed property searches and EC applications.

What is the IGRSUP Index?

The IGRSUP Index is a search option for deeds registered after 5 December 2017. It lets you search by address details such as tehsil, colony and mohalla name, so you can find a deed even without the khasra number or property ID. For deeds registered before that date, use the Sampatti Vivaran search by khasra or property ID, or ask at the Sub-Registrar Office.

Do I need to log in to search property on IGRSUP?

When we checked igrsup.gov.in on 17 October 2026, the property search links opened a User Login page (user ID, password and captcha). If you do not have an account, use the Register Now option on the home page first. Once logged in, choose rural or urban property and enter the district, tehsil, village and khasra number, or the district and property ID.
