<h2 id="mistakes" style="margin-top: 40px;">🚨 5 Biggest Mistakes Buyers Make on Raebareli Road</h2>

<p style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: bold; color: #011f4b; margin: 0 0 6px;">Buying Agricultural Land Because It's Cheap</p>
<p style="font-size: 13.5px; color: #374151; line-height: 1.75; margin: 0;">The most common trap in Zone C. A seller offers 2,000 sqft at ₹900/sqft and it sounds like a deal. But the Khatauni shows <strong>Krishi Bhoomi (agricultural)</strong>. You cannot build on it, bank will refuse a loan, and building plan approval will be rejected. <a href="https://dsdproperties.in/blog/transforming-agricultural-land-residential-property-lucknow" style="color:#008080;font-weight:600;">Land use conversion</a> (Dharanataran) can take 2–4 years and is not guaranteed. <div style="display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 14px;">

<p style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: bold; color: #011f4b; margin: 0 0 6px;">Trusting "Highway-Facing" as a Quality Signal</p>
<p style="font-size: 13.5px; color: #374151; line-height: 1.75; margin: 0;">Agents market plots as "main Raebareli Road" or "highway touch" to charge a premium. <p style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: bold; color: #011f4b; margin: 0 0 6px;">"Registry Ho Gayi = Safe Ho Gaya" — This is Wrong</p>
<p style="font-size: 13.5px; color: #374151; line-height: 1.75; margin: 0;">Many buyers believe once the sale deed is registered at the Sub-Registrar, the plot is legally clean. <strong>Incorrect.</strong> Registry only records a transaction — it does not validate the seller's title, land use, or layout approval. You can register an agricultural plot, an encumbered plot, or a disputed plot. The Sub-Registrar does not verify any of this. Legal safety requires Khatauni, <a href="https://dsdproperties.in/blog/encumbrance-certificate-lucknow-how-to-apply-online" style="color:#008080;font-weight:600;">Encumbrance Certificate</a>, LDA layout, and RERA check — <em>before</em> registry.</p>

<p style="font-size: 15px; font-weight: bold; color: #011f4b; margin: 0 0 6px;">Not Physically Checking Road Access to the Plot</p>
<p style="font-size: 13.5px; color: #374151; line-height: 1.75; margin: 0;">Dozens of plots in the Mohanlalganj–Kalli Pashchim belt have no legal road access. The approach road passes through another private party's land or a seasonal farm track. Under 2026 Compounding Policy, plots without 9-metre confirmed road access cannot get building plan approval. <strong>Physically trace the entire road from the main road to the plot boundary</strong> — not just the last 100 metres. Ask the seller to show it on the LDA layout map explicitly.</p>

<h2 id="FAQs" style="margin-top: 40px;">❓ Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>What is the actual plot price on Raebareli Road in 2026?</h3>
<p>Plot prices vary significantly by zone and legal status. In Zone A (PGI Belt — Vrindavan Yojna area), approved resale plots range from ₹2,500–₹5,500/sqft depending on road width and location. In Zone B (DLF Corridor), DLF Garden City BSP is ₹2,100–₹2,500/sqft; Omaxe Metro City plots are ₹4,500–₹5,000/sqft. In Zone C (Mohanlalganj–Kisan Path), approved societies range ₹1,200–₹2,000/sqft; unapproved land drops to ₹700–₹1,200/sqft. The ₹6,000+ figures you see on portals are blended apartment averages — not plot rates.</p>
<h3>Is Raebareli Road a good investment for plots in 2026?</h3>
<p>Yes — specifically Zone B (DLF Corridor) for 3–5 year investors, and Zone A (PGI Belt) for rental income buyers. DLF entry plots at ₹2,100/sqft against GNE plots at ₹5,000–₹10,000/sqft represents a 50–75% entry discount on a corridor with confirmed infrastructure (SGPGI expansion, SCR Vigyan Path, Kisan Path connectivity). The RRTS proposal adds further upside. However Zone C requires very careful verification — significant unapproved inventory exists.</p>
<h3>Which is better — DLF Garden City or Omaxe Metro City for plots?</h3>
<p>Different profiles suit different buyers. DLF Garden City has lower entry (₹2,100/sqft BSP vs ₹4,500/sqft for Omaxe plots) and larger plot sizes (2,700–4,500 sqft). Omaxe Metro City is closer to SGPGI (~1 km vs ~5 km for DLF) and offers smaller plot sizes from 1,200 sqft — better for buyers with ₹55–75L budgets. Both are LDA and RERA approved. For rental income: Omaxe wins on proximity to SGPGI. For long-term land appreciation: DLF wins on size and established township status.</p>
<h3>Can I get a home loan for a plot on Raebareli Road?</h3>
<p>Yes, for LDA-approved and <a href="https://dsdproperties.in/blog/understanding-rera-guide-lucknow-property-buyers-C7ECl" style="color:#008080;font-weight:600;">RERA-registered</a> plots. DLF Garden City, Omaxe Metro City, Suraksha Enclave, Skyom City, and Vrindavan Yojna (UPAVP) all have bank loan approvals from major lenders including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Unapproved Zila Panchayat plots and agricultural land are refused by all banks without exception — this is one of the most reliable ways to filter legal plots: if a bank won't lend on it, you shouldn't buy it.</p>
<h3>What is the minimum budget for a legal plot on Raebareli Road?</h3>
<p>For a legally safe, RERA or LDA approved plot, minimum realistic budget is ₹15–20 lakh for 1,000 sqft in Zone C approved societies or Vrindavan Yojna back-lane resale. For a gated township with full amenities (DLF, Suraksha, Omaxe), budget ₹47 lakh minimum. Below ₹15 lakh, almost all available plots are unapproved or agricultural — the legal risk at that price point is extremely high.</p>
<h3>Why are plot rates on portals showing ₹6,000/sqft for Raebareli Road?</h3>
<p>Portal averages like ₹6,050/sqft (99acres) or ₹6,000/sqft (Kalli Pashchim on 99acres) are blended averages covering all property types — apartments, flats, independent houses, and plots combined. Apartment rates in gated societies skew these averages significantly upward. Actual plot-only transaction data shows rates of ₹900–₹5,500/sqft depending on zone and approval status. Always ask for plot-specific comps from actual transactions, not corridor averages.</p>
<h3>What are the risks of buying a plot near SGPGI on Raebareli Road?</h3>
<p>The three main risks: (1) unapproved Zila Panchayat colonies sold as residential — no bank loan, demolition risk; (2) agricultural land sold as residential without conversion — cannot build, bank refuses financing; (3) "Registry ho gayi = safe" misconception — registration does not validate title, land use, or layout approval. Always verify RERA at up-rera.in, land use at upbhulekh.gov.in, and LDA layout at ldalucknow.in before paying anything.</p>
