What is Naimish Nagar Yojana?

Naimish Nagar Yojana is a large residential township being developed by Lucknow Development Authority (LDA). It is spread across ~3,681 acres and will provide 6,000+ residential plots. Plot pricing will be around ₹3,800 per square foot.

🏘️ Key Features of Naimish Nagar

  • ~3,681 acres area — in Bakshi ka Talab region
  • 6,000+ residential plots — for all income categories
  • ₹3,200/sqft price — 20-30% cheaper than nearby private societies
  • 2 lakh+ planned population — major urban expansion
  • Complete infrastructure development by LDA — roads, water, electricity, sewage
  • Modern amenities — schools, hospital, parks, markets

This scheme is being developed at an estimated cost of ₹4,785 crore. According to LDA, this scheme will give a new identity to the northern part of Lucknow and play an important role in the city's expansion.

📍 Location and Connectivity

Naimish Nagar Yojana is being developed in the Bakshi ka Talab area of Lucknow. This region is located on Sitapur Road and has good road connectivity with the city, though it is at a reasonable distance from the city center.

🛣️ Key Connectivity Points

  • Sitapur Road — direct access connecting to Lucknow city
  • Bakshi ka Talab area — previously no residential schemes existed here
  • Rapidly developing region — infrastructure improvements coming in coming years
  • Near government areas — close to Aliganj, Kanpur Road regions

Villages Under Acquisition

Naimish Nagar Yojana will be developed on land from 14 villages. These villages fall under Bakshi ka Talab (BKT) Tehsil. LDA is buying land from farmers on a consent basis (बैनामे से).

List of Villages (14 Villages)

  • Bhiti Group: Saipur, Kamalpur, Dhatingra, Umarbhari
  • Lakshmipur Group: Farrukhabad, Palhari, Saidapur, Duggaur
  • Purab Village Group: Kodri Bhiti, Gopramau, Paschim Village, Dhobaila
  • Purwa: Kamalabad, Barumau, Shobela

Important: This scheme does NOT use land pooling like IT City or Wellness City. LDA is directly paying farmers in cash. This is why the acquisition process can be completed quickly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy land in Naimish Nagar right now?

Yes, you can buy from private sellers in the Naimish Nagar area right now. But exercise caution — land verification is mandatory. LDA's official lottery hasn't opened yet, which will start in May 2026. Until then, private market transactions will continue.

What is the plot price in Naimish Nagar?

According to LDA's plan, plot prices in Naimish Nagar will be around ₹3,200 per square foot. Currently nearby private societies are selling land at ₹2,000-₹3,200/sqft. Official pricing will be announced after land blessing.

When will the land blessing happen?

Naimish Nagar Yojana's land blessing will happen in April 2026. After that, development work will start and RERA registration will be done.

When will registration open?

Public registration will open from May 2026 onwards. Applicants will be allotted plots through the official lottery process.

Is this a land pooling scheme?

No, Naimish Nagar is not a land pooling scheme. LDA is directly buying land from farmers through consent-based acquisition (बैनामे). Farmers are receiving cash payments, unlike IT City.

What precautions should I take before buying?

Always verify the title, check for encumbrances, and consult a lawyer. LDA acquisition is ongoing in the Naimish Nagar area, so be vigilant.

How many villages' land is included?
