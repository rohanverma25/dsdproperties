\ud83d\udcb0 What Decides Your Final Varun Vihar Plot Cost?

  • Plot category — EWS and LIG plots are priced lower; MIG and HIG plots cost more per square foot
  • Plot size — expect sizes from compact EWS plots up to large HIG plots, following LDA's standard pattern
  • Location within the scheme — corner plots, park-facing plots and main-road plots usually carry a premium
  • Registration and one-time charges — add freehold, lease rent, corner charges and registry/stamp duty on top of the base rate
  • Payment plan — LDA typically offers a lump-sum option and an instalment plan; instalments may carry interest

A realistic way to budget: take the per-square-foot rate, multiply by your plot size in square feet, then add roughly 7–10% for stamp duty and registry charges. Home loans are available on LDA plots from most nationalised and major private banks, since LDA is a UP government authority.
