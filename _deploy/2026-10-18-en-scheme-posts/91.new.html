The Lucknow Development Authority's (LDA) Varun Vihar Yojana is coming up near the Agra–Lucknow Expressway. ETV Bharat called it LDA's largest residential scheme so far. Online registration for 2,847 Phase 1 plots is open until 24 October 2026. This guide covers the location, villages, plot sizes and prices, payment terms and how to apply — all with official sources.

What is Varun Vihar Yojana?

According to LDA's website, Varun Vihar is planned on about 6,580 acres. It will have 25 sectors (with underground services) and more than 15,000 plots — residential, commercial, mixed use, institutional and group housing. (LDA)

When complete, the scheme is expected to house about 3 lakh people (ETV Bharat, September 2026); an April 2026 report put the figure at 4 lakh. LDA's layout plan (July 2025) names the scheme "Udyog Nagar (Agra Expressway) Housing Scheme" — it was later renamed Varun Vihar.

Where is Varun Vihar?

According to LDA's layout plan, the Agra–Lucknow Expressway lies to the north of the scheme and the Fatehganj–Mohan Road to the south. The Lucknow end of the expressway, where it meets the Outer Ring Road (Kisan Path), is about 8.7 km in a straight line from the middle of the scheme.

According to ETV Bharat (April 2026), an 11 km elevated road along the Haidar canal is proposed to link the scheme to the city, which would cut the trip to Hazratganj from about 50 minutes to 20 minutes. (ETV Bharat) This is still a proposal.

Payment, Instalments and Discounts

Payment terms (same for Varun Vihar and Naimish Nagar)

  • Booklet — ₹1,100
  • Registration deposit — 5% of the plot price (2.5% for reserved categories)
  • Instalments — 36 monthly instalments at 9.7% interest
  • Discount for full payment — 6% if paid in 45 days, 5% in 60 days, 4% in 90 days
  • Priority for full payment — their lottery is drawn first (Bhaskar Digital)
  • Freehold charge — 12%, paid at registry
  • Cancelling your registration — 20% is deducted

Source for terms: Amar Ujala, 24 September 2026

Eligibility and How to Apply

Registration is online only, on registration.ldalucknow.in. According to Amar Ujala, the applicant must be at least 18 years old and must not have been allotted a plot or house earlier by LDA or any housing authority. For reservation categories, income certificates and documents, read our LDA reservation and income certificate guide (in Hindi).

Please note

  • Register only on LDA's website. Do not pay any broker who says they can "get you an LDA plot".
  • Plots will be allotted by lottery. LDA will announce the lottery date.

What Is in the Master Plan

According to LDA's website and ETV Bharat reports, these facilities are proposed:

  • A logistics park of about 300 acres (LDA) — the layout plan shows Sector 15 as "Transport Nagar / non-polluting industry (MSME)" and a logistics hub.
  • Green area — LDA's website says more than 980 acres (parks, water bodies, golf course, sports facilities, central and sector parks); ETV Bharat (April 2026) reported 800 acres.
  • An international-standard golf course, a natural lake of about 70 acres and a sports hub (ETV Bharat)
  • 25 sectors with underground services (LDA)

Buying Private Land Near the Scheme? Check These First

These checks are for people buying land from a private seller around the scheme area. If you want an LDA plot, simply apply on registration.ldalucknow.in.

  • Is the land inside the Varun Vihar boundary (12 villages)? — Confirm with LDA; LDA is buying the scheme's land from farmers
  • Is the seller's name in the khatauni? — Check on upbhulekh.gov.in
  • Any loan or mortgage on it? — Get an encumbrance certificate (EC)
  • Check the chain of earlier registries — property search on IGRSUP
  • Is it farmland? — Without a Section 80 order, no building map will be passed on it
  • Is the dakhil-kharij (mutation) in the seller's name? — Check at the tehsil
  • Any court case? — Have a lawyer check

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Varun Vihar Yojana

What is the last date to apply for Varun Vihar Yojana?

Registration for the 2,847 Phase 1 plots is open from 24 September to 24 October 2026 on registration.ldalucknow.in.

What is the plot price in Varun Vihar?

LDA has fixed the rate at ₹2,800 per sq ft. A 112.5 sq m plot costs about ₹33.9 lakh and a 450 sq m plot about ₹1.36 crore. Corner/park-facing charges and the 12% freehold charge are extra.

How much money do I need to deposit to register?

After the ₹1,100 booklet, you deposit 5% of the plot price (2.5% for reserved categories). For a 112.5 sq m plot this is about ₹1.7 lakh.

Is there any benefit in paying the full amount at once?

Yes. Paying in full within 45 days gives a 6% discount, within 60 days 5% and within 90 days 4%. According to Bhaskar Digital, the lottery for full-payment applicants will be drawn first.

Where is Varun Vihar in Lucknow?

Near the Agra–Lucknow Expressway, in Sadar and Sarojini Nagar tehsils. The expressway lies to the north of the scheme and the Fatehganj–Mohan Road to the south.

Which villages are in Varun Vihar?

According to LDA, 12 villages: Bhaliya, Adampur Indwara, Bahru, Jaliyamau, Madarpur, Ibrahimganj, Naktaura, Gahlwara, Tejkrishna Khera, Rewari, Sakra and Dona.

Which blocks have plots in Phase 1?

Kashi Khand, Kailash Khand Phase 1 and Kailash Khand Phase 2 have 2,847 plots in total — 1,517 of 112.5 sq m, 460 of 162, 612 of 200, 68 of 288, 81 of 300 and 109 of 450 sq m.

Who can apply for Varun Vihar?

According to Amar Ujala, anyone aged 18 or above who has not been allotted a plot or house earlier by LDA or any housing authority. The full conditions are in LDA's booklet.
