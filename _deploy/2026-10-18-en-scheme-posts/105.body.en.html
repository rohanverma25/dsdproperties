Naimish Nagar Yojana (also written Naimish Nagar or Naimisharanya in the news) is a new residential scheme of the Lucknow Development Authority (LDA) near Sitapur Road and IIM Road in Bakshi ka Talab (BKT) tehsil. Registration for 1,673 Phase 1 plots is open until 24 October 2026. The LDA rate is ₹3,800 per sq ft.

The scheme is planned to house about 2 lakh people. LDA is buying land from farmers by consent — this is not a land pooling scheme like IT City or Wellness City. Farmers are paid in cash.

Important: To get an LDA plot, register only on registration.ldalucknow.in. Do not pay any broker or agent who offers to "get you an LDA plot". Private land is also being bought and sold around the scheme area — check its status with LDA before you buy such land.

What is Naimish Nagar Yojana?

Naimish Nagar Yojana is a large residential scheme of the Lucknow Development Authority (LDA), developed under the Chief Minister's Urban Expansion Scheme. According to Amar Ujala (23 September 2026), its total area is about 4,100 acres. Earlier reports gave 2,504 acres (Amar Ujala, July 2025) and about 3,000 acres (February 2026). Check the final figure in the LDA booklet.

🏘️ Key Features of Naimish Nagar

  • About 4,100 acres — in Bakshi ka Talab (BKT) tehsil
  • 1,673 plots in Phase 1 — six sizes: 72, 112.5, 162, 200, 288 and 450 sq m
  • ₹3,800 per sq ft — the rate fixed by LDA
  • About 2 lakh people — planned population
  • Full infrastructure by LDA — roads, water, electricity, sewer
  • Facilities — land is set aside for education, cultural and spiritual facilities

The estimated cost of the scheme is ₹4,785 crore (Amrit Vichar). It is LDA's plan to expand the city along Sitapur Road. For more on the area, read our Bakshi ka Talab guide.

📍 Location and Connectivity

Naimish Nagar Yojana is being developed in Bakshi ka Talab (BKT) tehsil of Lucknow, between Sitapur Road and Raitha Road, near IIM Road.

🛣️ Key Connectivity

  • Sitapur Road — direct road to the city
  • IIM Road and Raitha Road — the scheme area lies between these roads
  • Fast-growing area — infrastructure is expected to improve in the coming years

Villages and Land Purchase

The scheme was first planned on 1,084 hectares in 14 villages. In August 2025, 29 hectares from 4 more villages were added for the main roads, making 18 villages in total (Amrit Vichar). All the villages are in BKT tehsil. LDA is buying land from farmers by consent (through sale deeds, called bainama).

List of Villages (18 Villages)

  • Original 14 villages: Bhauli, Lakshmipur, Purab Gaon, Purwa, Sairpur, Farrukhabad, Kodri Bhauli, Kamalabad, Kamalapur, Palhari, Gopramau, Barumau, Dhatingra, Saidapur
  • 4 villages added in August 2025 (for roads): Paschim Gaon, Dhobaila, Umarbhari, Duggaur

Important: This scheme does not use the land pooling policy used in IT City and Wellness City. LDA pays farmers directly through registered sale deeds.

💰 Plot Price and Payment

₹3,800 per sq ft (LDA rate)

  • Registration deposit — 5% of the plot price (2.5% for reserved categories)
  • Booklet — ₹1,100
  • Instalments — 36 monthly instalments at 9.7% interest
  • Discount for full payment — 6% if paid in 45 days, 5% in 60 days, 4% in 90 days
  • Freehold charge — 12%, paid at registry
  • Cancelling your registration — 20% is deducted

Example: a 112.5 sq m plot (about 1,211 sq ft) at ₹3,800 will cost about ₹46 lakh (corner, park-facing and other charges are extra). To register, you deposit 5% of this, about ₹2.3 lakh. Check the exact price and charges in the LDA booklet. (Terms: Amar Ujala, 24 September 2026)

Eligibility and Application

Registration is online only, on registration.ldalucknow.in. According to Amar Ujala, the main conditions are:

📋 Eligibility and Application

  • The applicant must be at least 18 years old
  • The applicant must not have been allotted a plot or house earlier by LDA or any housing authority
  • First buy the ₹1,100 booklet, then deposit 5% of the plot price (2.5% for reserved categories)
  • The full conditions, reservations and plot list are in the booklet — read it before you apply

✅ Verification Before Buying

These checks are for people buying land from a private seller around the scheme area. You do not need them for a plot allotted by LDA. Always verify before you buy:

⚠️ 8-Point Verification Checklist

  • → Is the land inside the Naimish Nagar scheme area? — Check with LDA
  • → Is the seller's name in the khatauni? — Check on upbhulekh.gov.in
  • → Any encumbrance? — Get a certificate from the Sub-Registrar
  • → Is the dakhil-kharij (mutation) done? — Check at the tehsil
  • → Agricultural or residential? — Land for a house must be non-agricultural
  • → Any government claim? — Risk of LDA acquisition
  • → Any court case? — Possible legal problems
  • → Take a lawyer's opinion — always get professional advice

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for Naimish Nagar now?

Registration for the 1,673 Phase 1 plots is open from 24 September to 24 October 2026 on registration.ldalucknow.in. Buy the ₹1,100 booklet and deposit 5% of the plot price (2.5% for reserved categories). If you want to buy private land around the scheme area, get it verified first.

What is the plot price in Naimish Nagar?

LDA has fixed the Naimish Nagar rate at ₹3,800 per sq ft. For example, a 112.5 sq m plot (about 1,211 sq ft) will cost about ₹46 lakh. Corner, park-facing and freehold (12%) charges are extra.

When was Naimish Nagar Yojana launched?

The scheme launched on 24 September 2026, together with Varun Vihar Yojana. In February 2026 the plan was ground-breaking in April and registration from May, but the launch was delayed several times.

What is the last date for registration?

The last date is 24 October 2026. LDA will announce the allotment date later.

Is this a land pooling scheme?

No. LDA is buying land directly from farmers by consent, through sale deeds (bainama). IT City and Wellness City use land pooling; Naimish Nagar does not.

What precautions should I take before buying?

Always check the khatauni, check for encumbrances and take a lawyer's advice. LDA is buying land in the scheme area, so be careful with private deals there.

How many villages are included?

18 villages in total. The original 14 are Bhauli, Lakshmipur, Purab Gaon, Purwa, Sairpur, Farrukhabad, Kodri Bhauli, Kamalabad, Kamalapur, Palhari, Gopramau, Barumau, Dhatingra and Saidapur. Paschim Gaon, Dhobaila, Umarbhari and Duggaur were added in August 2025 for roads.
