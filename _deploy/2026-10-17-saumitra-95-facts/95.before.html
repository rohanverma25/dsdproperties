What is Saumitra Vihar Yojana?

Saumitra Vihar is a planned residential township on New Jail Road, in the Gosainganj–Mohanlalganj area of Lucknow. It is being developed by the Uttar Pradesh Awas Vikas Parishad, also called the UP Housing and Development Board.

The project got its name fairly recently. In December 2024, the Parishad announced a new residential scheme on Lucknow’s New Jail Road. Then in the 270th board meeting in March 2025, the board formally approved the name — Saumitra Vihar Yojana.

One thing people get confused about, so let us clear it up straight away:

This is UPAVP, not LDA

  • IT City, Wellness City, Anant Nagar and Varun Vihar are LDA schemes (Lucknow Development Authority).

  • You will apply on upavp.in for this one — not on the LDA portal.
  • Both are government organisations, so the plots are equally genuine. Just do not mix up the two portals when registration opens.

How land pooling works here

This is the part that makes Saumitra Vihar special, and it is worth understanding properly — especially if you own land anywhere on the outskirts of Lucknow, because this model is likely to be repeated.

Normally, when the government needs land for a township, it acquires it. A notice is issued, a compensation amount is fixed, and the farmer gets money whether he agrees with the price or not. You know what usually happens next — court cases, stay orders, and projects stuck for ten years.

Land pooling works differently. The farmer is not forced. He gives his land willingly, and instead of only cash, he gets a share of the same land back — but now developed, with roads, drainage and electricity, and with clean papers. Agricultural land that was worth very little suddenly becomes a proper residential plot he can sell or build on.

What has already happened with the farmers

  • Farmers gave land willingly. There was no forced acquisition notice in this scheme.
  • The registry is complete. The land has been formally transferred into the Parishad’s name.
  • 192 farmers have already received developed plots through a lottery draw.
  • Because everyone agreed, there is very little chance of a land dispute surfacing later.
  • This is the first township in Uttar Pradesh to be built this way.

Why should you care as a buyer? Simple. Most plot frauds and most stuck projects in UP come from land disputes. Someone challenges the acquisition, a court puts a stay, and your money sits blocked for years. When land comes through pooling with everyone’s consent and the registry already done, that whole category of risk mostly goes away.

It does not mean you skip verification. It just means the most common problem is much less likely here.

How to apply, step by step

When registration opens, this is how it will work. Everything happens online on the official portal — there is no counter booking and no agent channel.

1

Keep checking upavp.in

The announcement will appear in the schemes section of the official UPAVP website. The brochure and price list will come out at the same time. Read both properly before deciding anything.

2

Check the RERA number first

Once the brochure is out, take the RERA number from it and check it yourself on up-rera.in. If the brochure has no RERA number, stop and wait.

3

Create your account

Register as a new user with your own mobile number and verify with OTP. Please use your own number and email, never an agent’s.

4

Choose your plot size carefully

Look at the plot count table above. 75 sq m has the most plots but the most applicants. 300 sq m has only 161 plots. Balance what you can afford against your chances.

5

Fill the form and upload documents

Aadhaar, PAN, photograph and address proof. Add an income certificate if you are applying under a reserved income category. Make sure your name is spelled the same everywhere.

6

Pay the registration amount online

Only through the UPAVP portal. Save the receipt and transaction number. Never pay cash to anyone for this.

7

Wait for the lottery

Allotment happens through a computerised draw and results go up on the portal. Nobody can influence it, and nobody can promise you a plot beforehand.

8

Download your allotment letter

If your name comes up, download the letter from your own UPAVP dashboard. That document is what makes every future payment valid.

9

Pay instalments on time

Follow the schedule in your allotment letter. Late payments attract interest, and repeated delays can lead to cancellation.

10

Possession and registry

After full payment and development, UPAVP hands over possession. Get the sale deed done at the Sub-Registrar office and then complete mutation in your name.

Stay safe from fraud

This is the most important section of this article, so please read it carefully. The period before a government scheme opens is exactly when fraud happens, because people are excited and there is no official information to check against.

Nobody can book a Saumitra Vihar plot for you right now

  • Registration has not opened. There is no form to fill.
  • There is no advance booking system in this scheme. None. It does not exist.
  • There is no special quota, VIP quota or priority booking anyone can arrange.
  • Nobody can guarantee you a plot. It is a computer lottery — it cannot be fixed.
  • UPAVP takes money only through its own website. Cash, UPI to a personal number, or a cheque to an individual means fraud.
  • The only official website is upavp.in. A website with the scheme name in its address is not an official website.

Seven checks before you pay anything

  1. Is the registration notice actually on upavp.in? Type the address yourself. Do not open links forwarded on WhatsApp.
  2. Does the brochure have a RERA number, and does it match on up-rera.in? Check it on the RERA site yourself. A number printed on paper proves nothing.
  3. Is the rate you are quoted the official brochure rate? Anything noticeably higher means someone has added their own margin.
  4. Are you paying only through the UPAVP website? No cash, no personal UPI, no agent’s account, ever.
  5. Is someone promising guaranteed allotment? That one sentence is proof of fraud. End the conversation there.
  6. Do your documents match each other? Name spelling on Aadhaar, PAN and address proof must be the same, or your form can be rejected after the draw.
  7. Did your allotment letter come from your own UPAVP dashboard? Check it before paying any instalment. Fake allotment letters are the most common plot fraud in Lucknow.
