Key Facts (22 October 2026)

  • Shalimar Corp lists 8 ongoing residential and 2 commercial projects on its website: 7 in the Lucknow area and 1 in Varanasi.
  • They range from 2 BHK homes on Ayodhya Road (Mannat Extension 3, Garden Bay Crown) to 4 BHK-only residences in Mahanagar (Gallant West) and 22-floor towers (Sky Garden).
  • Every project page shows a UP RERA number. The newest is Evara Heights (UPRERAPRJ908475/05/2026).
  • No prices are published on the project pages. Two projects need a closer look before booking: Evara, where Shalimar's own pages disagree on plots versus apartments, and Gallant West, whose RERA completion date is 20 Oct 2026.

Shalimar is one of Lucknow's oldest developers, and its projects sit in very different parts of the city: Mahanagar, IIM Road, Gomti Nagar Extension, Ayodhya Road, and now Varanasi. The official website gives each project its own page, but you can't see them side by side or on one map. This guide does that. It puts every ongoing Shalimar project on one interactive map, compares them in one table, and lists the checks to make before you book. What the official website doesn't tell you

  1. Prices. No ongoing project page shows a price. Ask for a dated price sheet that lists BSP, PLC, parking, club and maintenance separately.
  2. Evara: plots or flats? The listing page says "14.23 acres villa plots". The project page says 2 & 3 BHK apartments in 4 towers of up to G+6. Settle this from the RERA certificate.
  3. Gallant West dates. The RERA record showed completion due 20 Oct 2026 and 69 flats. Shalimar's page says 71 families. Ask for the extension order, if any.
  4. Barabanki vs Lucknow. Mannat Extension 3 is in Barabanki district, so the registry office, circle rate and civic body all differ from Lucknow.
  5. Shared registrations. Sky Garden's apartments and retail street share one RERA number (UPRERAPRJ160941), filed under the commercial category.

Before you book any Shalimar home

  1. Search the project's RERA number on up-rera.in and note the promoter, the completion date and the latest quarterly report.
  2. Match the tower, floor and unit in your allotment letter to the RERA-registered plan.
  3. Take the builder-buyer agreement home before you pay more than 10%, and read the possession and delay-penalty clauses.
  4. For ready or resale homes, ask for the completion or occupancy certificate and an encumbrance certificate.
  5. For plots, check the approved layout and khasra: how to check an approved map.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many ongoing projects does Shalimar Corp list?

As of 22 October 2026, shalimarcorp.com lists eight ongoing residential projects and two commercial ones. The residential projects are Shalimar OneWorld (a township with several phases), Evara Heights, Evara, Sky Garden, Gallant West, Garden Bay Crown, Mannat Extension 3 and Courtyard in Varanasi. The commercial projects are Shalimar Business District Tower 1 and Sky Garden Retail Street.

Which Shalimar project has only 4 BHK homes?

Shalimar Gallant West in Mahanagar is a 4 BHK-only project with 2 towers. Shalimar says 71 families will live there, while the RERA record we checked shows 69 flats.

Which Shalimar projects are on Ayodhya Road?

Evara, Evara Heights and Mannat Extension 3 are on the Lucknow–Ayodhya Road. Mannat Extension 3 is in Barabanki district. Sky Garden and its retail street are near Polytechnic, at the city end of Ayodhya Road.

Does Shalimar publish prices on its website?

No. None of the ongoing project pages on shalimarcorp.com shows a price. For OneWorld, our separate guide lists projects, unit types and possession dates. Ask the developer for a dated price sheet, and compare it with the agreement before you pay.

Is Shalimar Evara a plotted project or apartments?

Shalimar's website says both. The listing page calls Evara "14.23 acres villa plots", while the project page shows 2 & 3 BHK apartments in 4 towers of up to G+6. Read the RERA certificate for UPRERAPRJ742268/11/2025 to see what is registered.

How do I check a Shalimar project's RERA status?

Search the RERA number on up-rera.in and read four things: the promoter name, the proposed completion date, any extension orders and the latest quarterly progress report. We also do this as part of our ₹5,000 property verification.
