# Sadar Pratham (SRO 227) rate list, effective 01-08-2025: rules transcribed from PDF pages 3+

Order no. ९७१ /स्टाम्प लिपिक, कार्यालय जिलाधिकारी लखनऊ, dated 01.08.2025. Under UP Stamp (Valuation of Property) Rules 1997, 2nd amendment 2013, 3rd amendment 2015, rule 4. Rates are the MINIMUM value for stamp duty.

PDF p3 (1):
1. Every deed must state which part / page / serial / column of this list the minimum value was computed from.
2. If a road is in the boundary (चौहद्दी), its width and common name must be stated.
3(क). Agricultural land transfer: attach latest khasra + khatauni / kisan bahi, form (प्रपत्र) 11 and affidavit.
3(ख). Geo-tagged photo with lat/long of the property must be attached (agri and non-agri).

PDF p4 (2):
4(क). Park facing: +10%. Plot with more than one road on its boundary: use the higher road rate, then +10%. Both (park + two roads): +20%.
4(ख). Non-agri plot/building with a commercial/business activity in its boundary: plot rate +20%.
5. Deed needs: route map (नक्शा नजरी); for built property the full construction map with built area; party names, father/husband, house no., street, mohalla, postal address, phones and ID numbers of buyer/seller/witnesses; name/address/mobile of map drafter. Previous agreement (इकरारनामा) registration details if any.
6. Basement = floor fully below ground. Half below / part above counts as ground floor.

PDF p5 (3):
7(क). Multi-storey (more than 3 floors) non-commercial buildings (part 3, form 5 ख): value = construction rate + proportionate land value. Proportionate land area in a flat is not less than 30% of its covered area.
7(ख)(I) Floors 4–6: −5%. (II) 7th floor and above: −10%. (III) Penthouse: no rebate. (IV) Exit on main road or its service lane: all units at the main-road rate. (V) Premises on two or more roads: proportionate land at the highest road rate +10%. (VI) Covered parking sold separately: valued at the RCC construction rate for up-to-3-floor non-commercial buildings.

PDF p6 (4):
8. A revenue village / mohalla missing from the list: use the rate of the NEAREST listed village/mohalla.
9. Where extra stamp duty applies under any act, the deed must say whether the property is inside or outside that limit.
10. Building of 2–3 floors, floor-wise transfer: undivided land share 50% each (2 floors) or 33.3% (3 floors). Sale of roof rights (छत), minimum value = non-agri land rate × : ground-floor roof 1/2, first-floor roof 1/3, second 1/4, third 1/5, then 1/6, 1/7…
11. Soil for excavation ₹80 per cubic metre. Running brick kiln: ₹60 lakh per chimney.

PDF p7 (5):
12(अ). Agricultural land in URBAN-area villages/mohallas: up to 0.100 ha → at the non-agri rate (part 2). 0.100–0.1500 ha → first 0.100 ha at non-agri rate + rest at 70% of non-agri rate. Above 0.1500 ha → the above for the first 0.1500 ha + rest at the agricultural rate for that place.
12(ब). Agricultural land in SEMI-URBAN villages adjoining a pucca road / RCC / kharanja or abadi/buildings/residential activity: up to 0.050 ha at the non-agri rate; above 0.050 ha the rest at the agricultural rate.

PDF p8 (6):
13. Beyond the limits in 12अ cat 1–2 and 12ब cat 1, remaining agri land adjoining both road and abadi: road rate +40%. Agri land 50–200 m from the gata adjoining abadi: (general or road) agri rate +30%. "Adjoining abadi" = abadi/buildings/residential activity within a 50 m radius.
14. Other villages (not the listed urban/semi-urban ones): gata 50–200 m from abadi-adjoining gata → agri rate +30%; gata on a road with abadi within 50 m → road agri rate +40%.
15. Borewell/submersible ₹30,000 each; well ₹10,000; tap/hand pump ₹5,000.

PDF p9 (7) Industrial:
16(क). UPSIDC / govt-developed industrial plots: Amausi 4,500; Gahru 5,000; Harchandpur Garhikanaura/Garhikanaura/Talkatora 6,000; Chinhat 6,000; Dhawan 5,000; Defence Industrial Corridor Bhatgaon 1,700; Sarojini Nagar 4,500 (₹/sq m).
16(ख). Other industrial units inside Nagar Nigam limits (cement, rice/flour/sugar mill, cold storage, kiln, warehouse…): non-agri plot rate −20%.
16(ग). Outside Nagar Nigam, up to 1,000 sq m: non-agri plot rate −20%. (घ) Outside Nagar Nigam, above 1,000 sq m: non-agri rate −40%.

PDF p10 (8):
16(ङ). Industrial construction: RCC 12,000; RBC 10,000; tin shed/asbestos 6,000; khaprail 2,000 (₹/sq m). (च) Machinery: valued by a registered valuer's report.
17. The non-agri plot rate applies up to 1,000 sq m. The area above 1,000 sq m is valued at the plot rate −30%.
18. Property on the service lane of a segment road (form 3) or main road counts as on that road. Properties on the roads listed in form 3 are valued at that road-segment rate.
19. Flats in a complex with any two of: swimming pool, club, gym, segment road → premium flat category (premium construction rate). Otherwise ordinary (साधारण).
20. Whole multi-storey non-commercial building transferred: value = land + covered-area value.

PDF p11 (9): 21. Tree values by girth/diameter (cm) – table of 13 species classes (not needed for the page).

PDF p12 (10) Commercial – general:
C1. Single shop / commercial establishment sold with land: land at non-agri rate +50% + construction value.
C2. Construction rates for single shop/commercial: shop 22,000; office 18,000; godown & other 14,000 (₹/sq m).
C3. Shops/units inside a larger commercial building: by carpet area at the minimum rate (₹/sq m) set in this list.
C4. AC shops/units, and shops in shopping complex/mall/multiplex: +20% on the full value.
C5. Rates for units in non-single commercial buildings are for ground / upper ground / lower ground. Basement or 1st floor: 90%. 2nd, 3rd, 4th floor: 80%. Higher: 70%. This rebate does NOT apply to AC shops / shopping complex / mall / multiplex.

PDF p13 (11):
C6. Exit of the commercial premises on the main road or its service lane → all units at the main-road rate.
C7. Hotel / nursing home / guest house / marriage lawn / dhaba / cinema: non-agri land rate +50%; construction RCC 20,000, RBC 18,000, tin/asbestos & other 10,000 (₹/sq m).
C8. Commercial plot (deed says commercial plot, or commercial land in an approved layout/master plan): non-agri rate +50%.

PDF p14 (12) Part 3, Form 5क – construction rates, non-commercial buildings up to 3 floors (₹/sq m of covered area; life in years):
RCC (80 yr): first class 18,000 / second class 15,000
RBC (60 yr): 12,000 / 10,000
Kadi, girder-patiya, dat (50 yr): 6,000 / 5,000
Tin shed, asbestos, fibre shed (40 yr): 4,500 / 4,000
Kachcha, chhappar, khaprail (30 yr): 2,500 / 2,000
First class = doors/windows of sheesham/teak/deodar/compressed waterproof board/metal AND floor of marble/stone/vitrified tiles. Second class = anything else.
Building value = land (incl. open land) at the part-2 rate (form 2, 3 or 4) + construction value.

PDF p15 (13): Depreciation (IG Registration circular 1979/शि०का०लख०/2002 dated 19.09.2002):
construction value = covered area × rate; value for stamp = construction value − depreciation;
depreciation = construction value × actual age × 9 / (total life × 10).
Scrap value never below 30% of construction value. NO depreciation for the first 20 years from construction. Proof/affidavit of construction date and a registered draftsman's map (3 copies) required.

PDF p16+ (page-14…): Part 1, Form 1क = index of urban mohallas (name, ward, boundary, V-code). Same names as Form 2.
- Form 4 row 37 कायमखेड़ा prints 9–12 m = 22,500 but 12–18 m = 17,000 and 18 m+ = 19,000 (higher width lower rate). Shown exactly as printed, with a note.
- Form 4 row 93 बरहा कालोनी prints 12–18 m = 13,000 and 18 m+ = 13,000 (same). Shown as printed.
