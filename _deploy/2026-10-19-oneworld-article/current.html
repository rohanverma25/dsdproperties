What is Shalimar OneWorld?

Shalimar OneWorld is an integrated township — homes, villas, offices, a club and open spaces planned together — at Village Baghamau, Gomti Nagar Extension, near Amar Shaheed Path. The official Shalimar OneWorld website describes it as a township on the banks of the Gomti, built around a central lake. It is built in phases, and each phase has its own UP RERA registration, so a buyer should always check the certificate of the exact phase and tower.

Who you are buying from

The UP RERA certificates show that most OneWorld phases are registered by Shalimar Lakecity Private Limited (formerly ANS Developers Private Limited), with its office at Shalimar Titanium, Vibhuti Khand, Gomti Nagar. Twenty One is registered by Shalimar Corp Ltd. Both are Shalimar Group companies — make sure your agreement and receipts are in the name on your phase's certificate.

Super built-up area vs carpet area — what you actually get

Prices are per sq ft of super built-up area (SBUA), which includes walls and a share of lobbies, lifts and common areas. Carpet area is the floor inside your home. On this sheet, carpet area is roughly 58–75% of SBUA — for example, the Marbella 2 BHK + Study is 1,530 sq ft SBUA but 964.67 sq ft carpet (63%), while the Valencia County East villa is 6,530 sq ft SBUA and 4,846 sq ft carpet (74%). Compare projects on price per sq ft of carpet area, and make sure your agreement states the RERA carpet area.

Before you book in Shalimar OneWorld

  1. Get the full cost sheet — BSP plus every other charge, GST, stamp duty and registration, in writing.
  2. Check your phase's RERA certificate — number, promoter and end date — on up-rera.in, not only in the brochure.
  3. Agreement in the right name — Shalimar Lakecity Pvt Ltd or Shalimar Corp Ltd, exactly as on the certificate.
  4. Carpet area in the agreement — as per RERA, and see the "+ S" room on the floor plan.
  5. Construction progress — the official site publishes construction updates; compare them with the RERA quarterly report before each instalment.
  6. Resale in completed phases — ask for the registered sale deed, society dues clearance and an encumbrance certificate.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Shalimar OneWorld?

Shalimar OneWorld is an integrated township at Village Baghamau, Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow, near Amar Shaheed Path. It is developed by the Shalimar Group; most phases are registered by Shalimar Lakecity Pvt Ltd.

Which Shalimar OneWorld projects are on sale now?

Shalimar's price sheet effective 1 August 2026 lists Pinnacle, Marbella, Twenty One, Valencia Towers 2, Valencia Tower, Valencia County East, Valencia County East Phase 2, Whispering Woods, Belvedere Suites and Shalimar Business District (offices).

What is the price per sq ft in Shalimar OneWorld?

From 1 August 2026 the basic sale price (BSP) ranges from ₹9,000 per sq ft of super built-up area (Marbella 2 BHK + Study and 3 BHK) to ₹12,000 per sq ft (Pinnacle). Offices in Shalimar Business District are ₹11,000 per sq ft. Other charges, GST, stamp duty and registration are extra.

Which is the cheapest unit in Shalimar OneWorld?

On the 1 August 2026 sheet, the lowest basic cost is the Marbella 2 BHK + Study: 1,530 sq ft super built-up area at ₹9,000 per sq ft, a basic cost of about ₹1.38 crore before other charges.

Who is the developer of Shalimar OneWorld?

Shalimar OneWorld is a Shalimar Group township. Most phases are registered on UP RERA by Shalimar Lakecity Pvt Ltd (formerly ANS Developers Pvt Ltd); Twenty One is registered by Shalimar Corp Ltd. Your agreement should name the same company as your phase's RERA certificate.

Are Whispering Woods and Belvedere Suites delayed?

What does "+ S" mean in the unit type?

The price sheet writes units like "3 BHK + S" — an additional room beyond the bedrooms. Its exact use and size are in the floor plan; ask the sales team to show it before booking.
