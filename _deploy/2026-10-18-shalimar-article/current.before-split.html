Shalimar OneWorld, Gomti Nagar Extension — how it is set up

Shalimar OneWorld is an integrated township at Village Baghamau, Gomti Nagar Extension, near Amar Shaheed Path (Realty Doctors). It is not one RERA project: each phase — such as Valencia County, Marbella, Pinnacle, Twenty One, Whispering Woods, Belvedere Suites and Belvedere Court, all listed on the Shalimar Corp website — is registered separately.

Two points matter for buyers. First, published figures for the township's total size differ (about 200, 212 and 225 acres have all appeared), so ask for the approved layout of your phase rather than relying on a headline number (Realty Doctors). Second, the RERA promoter can be a group company: Whispering Woods (GH-14) is registered by M/s Shalimar Lakecity Private Limited, formerly M/s ANS Developers Private Limited (RERA record). Your builder–buyer agreement should name the same company as the RERA record of your phase.

Why some "Lucknow" projects are registered in Barabanki

Mannat and Paradise are marketed on Faizabad Road, Lucknow, but their land is in Nawabganj tehsil of Barabanki district, and RERA lists them there. In practice this means the sale deed is registered at the Barabanki sub-registrar office and Barabanki's circle rates apply. Make sure your documents and stamp duty calculation use the correct district.

Buying a Shalimar flat or plot? Check these first

  1. RERA number of your exact phase — a township can have many. Search it on up-rera.in.
  2. Promoter name — it must match the company in your builder–buyer agreement and on the payment receipts.
  3. Completion date and extensions — compare the RERA date with the possession date in your agreement.
  4. Quarterly progress report — RERA promoters file construction progress; read the latest one for your tower.
  5. Carpet area — the agreement should state carpet area as per RERA, not only super area.
  6. For resale in a completed project — ask for the completion/occupancy certificate, the seller's registered sale deed, maintenance dues clearance, and an encumbrance certificate; check past registries on IGRSUP.
  7. District — for Faizabad Road projects, confirm whether the land is in Lucknow or Barabanki.

Where is Shalimar OneWorld?

Is Shalimar Mannat in Lucknow or Barabanki?

Shalimar Mannat is marketed on Faizabad Road, Lucknow, but UP RERA lists Mannat Phase-1 (UPRERAPRJ8345) in Barabanki district, Nawabganj tehsil (RERA record). Property is registered at the sub-registrar office of the district where it lies.

Is Shalimar Gallant West RERA registered?

How do I check the RERA status of a Shalimar project?

Go to up-rera.in, search the project by its RERA number, and check four things: the promoter name, the completion date and any extension, the latest quarterly progress report, and the carpet area of your unit type. All four must match your agreement.
